JumpToTech Bank — VPC subnet design
====================================

VPC = 10.30.0.0/16

Allocate four consecutive, non-overlapping /20 networks inside that VPC.
Start at the first address of the VPC and work upwards, filling the lines
below in the order they appear. Write each one in CIDR notation.

AZ-A
  az_a_public = 
  az_a_private = 

AZ-B
  az_b_public = 
  az_b_private = 

No two of these four may cover the same address. If you can write one address
that falls inside two of your blocks, the allocation is wrong.
