JumpToTech Bank — ledger-api is "done", and it is down
======================================================

The deploy is finished and the engineer who did it has evidence: they ran the
health check on the box and got a 200. They ran it again while the incident
call was open and got a 200 again.

Every other machine gets connection refused, and the load balancer has taken
the service out of rotation.

Both of those are true at the same time. The service is healthy, it is
answering, and it is answering to exactly one address — which is not one
anybody else can use.


WHAT IS HERE
------------

  ledger-api                  the service, supervised, running
  /etc/ledger/api.conf        its configuration
  incident/evidence/          somewhere to put what you observe

There is one other machine on this segment. You cannot log into it — it is not
yours — but it is there, and whether it can reach this service is the question
the incident is actually about.


WHAT TO DO
----------

First reproduce both results, because a diagnosis nobody can check is an
opinion. Save what you get from this host into `evidence/local.txt`. Then work
out what the socket table says about *where* the service is listening, and why
that explains a refusal from anywhere else.

Then repair it: make the service reachable from the segment rather than only
from this host, and restart it through the supervisor so the change takes
effect. `sv restart ledger-api` and `sv status ledger-api` are the two forms
you are likely to want.

Finally write `diagnosis.txt`, in whatever words you like, containing:

  · the word `refused`, because that is what the other machine got, and
  · the word `bind`, because that is what was wrong

A refusal is not a timeout, and the difference tells you whether anything was
listening at all. That distinction is the reason this took an hour instead of
five minutes.
